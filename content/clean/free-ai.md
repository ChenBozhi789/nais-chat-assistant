# Free AI Engines

## Text Creation

### Chat GPT

ChatGPT is best used for providing information, answering questions, and assisting with a wide range of text-based tasks in a conversational manner.

[Get Started](https://chat.openai.com/)

### Gemini

Gemini is good for research, getting up-to-date reccomendations and advice.

[Get Started](https://gemini.google.com/app)

### Claude

Claude AI is best used for understanding and generating human-like text, assisting with writing, and can "read" PDF files.

[Get Started](https://claude.ai/)

### Perplexity

Perplexity has a great chrome extension that can look over articles and websites, and also is great for any kind of research.

[Get Started](https://www.perplexity.ai/)

### Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft Copilot is similar to Gemini in that it is great for research, but also has a variety of different tools that can easily be used alongside it to adjust its response.

[Get Started](https://copilot.microsoft.com/)

### Explain Like I'm Five

Explain Like I'm Five is a great way to get a simple answer to a quick question.

[Get Started](https://explainlikeimfive.io/)

### Character AI

Character AI is a fun tool to play with as you can have conversations with a huge variety of different people, fictional or real.

[Get Started](https://beta.character.ai/)

### SciSpace

SciSpace is a great tool for research - it summarises and provides insights from scientific papers based on your question.

[Get Started](https://typeset.io/)

### Autobiographer

Autobiographer will write an autobiography for you based on the conversation you have with it.

[Get Started](https://www.autobiographer.com/?utm_source=theneuron)

### Zapier

Zapier is good for automating repetitive tasks, improving productivity, and integrating different web applications seamlessly.

[Get Started](https://zapier.com/)

### [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/)

Mistral AI develops AI technologies for improving efficiency and automating tasks in areas like NLP, data analysis, computer vision, healthcare, finance, customer service, cybersecurity, supply chain, and education.

[Get Started](https://mistral.ai/)

### KREA

KREA specializes in generating high-quality AI visuals for creative design, content creation, entertainment, personal projects, education, and prototyping.

[Get Started](https://www.krea.ai/home)

### [Merlin AI](https://www.getmerlin.in/)

Merlin AI specializes in natural language processing and machine learning to provide insights and automate tasks, making it a valuable tool for businesses and developers. The Merlin AI Chrome add-on allows users to summarize YouTube videos quickly and efficiently - adding time stamps to the summary when 'Summarize this video' is chosen from the Window not the Widget!

[Get Started](https://www.getmerlin.in/)

## Free AI Engines

## Visual Creation.

### Leonardo

Leonardo is a simple and easy to use image generation tool.

[Get Started](http://leonardo.ai/)

### Microsoft Image Creator

Like Leonardo, Microsoft Image Creator is an easy to use image generation tool, and is also built inside Microsoft Copilot.

[Get Started](https://designer.microsoft.com/image-creator)

### Magic Eraser

Magic Eraser is an incredible tool for removing objects from images.

[Get Started](https://magicstudio.com/magiceraser/)

### Leiapix

Leia Pix is a very cool AI tool that can animate images.

[Get Started](https://www.leiapix.com/)

### Blockade Labs

Blockade Labs can generate a 360 degree view of an entire world.

[Get Started](https://skybox.blockadelabs.com/?_gl=1*17byauc*_ga*Nzc0NjEzNzgyLjE3MDU2Mjc3NTg.*_ga_YQXS5JLFDH*MTcwNTYyNzc1OC4xLjAuMTcwNTYyNzc1OC42MC4wLjA.)

### Lumalabs.ai

Lumalabs can create a 3d model of any object.

[Get Started](https://lumalabs.ai/genie?view=create)

### Ideogram

Ideogram is an image generation tool that is great at putting words and text into its images.

[Get Started](https://ideogram.ai/t/trending)

### AI Image Upscaler

Upscaler is a tool that can improve the quality of photos.

[Get Started](https://www.upscale.media/)

### Lumalabs

Lumalabs is one of the first publicly available text-to-video tools - with a text prompt, you can create an entirely AI generated video.

[Get Started](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine)

### Invideo

From a text prompt, invideo will create a video with a script and voiceover - the script and viceover are entirely AI generated, but the video is pulled from stock video.

[Get Started](https://ai.invideo.io/workspace/2cd8f7df-7fd8-4480-9b28-8a8cf093ac61/v20-copilot)

### Websim

From a url prompt, websim will create an AI generated webpage.

[Get Started](https://websim.ai/)

### Photoroom

Edit any image to remove unwanted fingers, legs or other objects

[Get Started](https://www.photoroom.com/tools/remove-object-from-photo)
