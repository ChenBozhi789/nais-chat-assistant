### Firefly Image Editor (2026) — the four generative tools

Adobe's Firefly Image Editor ships four generative tools inside Photoshop 2026. Each replaces a slow manual technique with a prompt.
Generative Fill
Add or replace anything inside a selection. The 2026 version is
geometry-aware — added objects match the scale, rotation and
lighting of the scene.
Generative Remove
One-click removal of distractions: power lines, tourists, dustbins,
lens flares. Cleans up edges where the old Spot Healing Brush
would smudge.
Generative Expand
Drag the crop handle outward and Firefly invents the rest of the
scene. Save a portrait that was framed too tight without re-
shooting.
Generative Upscale
Turn a 24 MP frame into a print-ready 100 MP file with believable
fine detail — fabric weave, eye texture, hair.

## Page 19

### Disclosure, copyright & consent in 2026

Disclosure
When an image is materially altered by AI, say so. Major stock libraries,
news outlets and competitions now require an “AI used” flag at
submission. Adobe embeds Content Credentials (C2PA) in every Firefly
export.
Copyright
In NZ, AI-generated work has unsettled status. The safer path:
substantially edit AI output before commercial release, keep your
prompt history, and credit Firefly Image 4 / FLUX.2 Pro / Nano Banana
Pro where it does serious image work.
Consent
Likeness rights still apply. Do not generate a recognisable real person
without permission. Use Nano Banana Pro's image consistency on
yourself or paid talent — not on someone in a crowd shot.
EU AI Act
Fully applicable from 2 August 2026, with the AI Act Omnibus (political
agreement July 2026) adding rules on AI-generated intimate content. If
you sell into the EU, know the deepfake and disclosure rules.
