### Perplexity — answers, with sources

Perplexity is an AI-powered answer engine. It
searches the web in real time, then uses a large
language model to summarise what it finds — citing
every source.
Why people use it
- Every answer cites its sources — easy to verify
- Live web search — current, not just training data
- Suggested follow-up questions to dig deeper
- Free to use; a paid Pro tier adds more models

## Page 10

### Explain Like I'm Five — a one-trick wonder

Explain Like I'm Five does one thing well: it takes a
topic and explains it in simple language. No long
onboarding, no settings to learn — just a prompt box
and a complexity slider.
When to reach for it
- You hit a concept you've never seen before
- You want a quick mental model, not a deep dive
- You're explaining something to a friend or child
- You need a starting point before reading more
THE C O MP L E XITY S L ID E R
Below the prompt box you'll find a slider that tells
the AI how high-level you want the answer.
Like a 5-year-old Normal Smart Dumb (deeper)
"Dumb" is misleading — it actually unlocks a deeper,
more detailed answer. "Like a 5-year-old" gives you
the gentlest summary.

Until 2024, Copilot just answered questions. In 2025

booking, filling forms, switching between apps.
Microsoft calls these "Copilot agents".
Two types you'll meet
- Built-in agents — vacation planner, cooking assistant,
fitness coach, image creator
- Custom agents — built in Copilot Studio for your team's
specific workflow

## Page 17

### Three things to keep in mind

1
AI can be wrong, confidently
Every tool here can produce "hallucinations" — answers that sound right but aren't. Cross-check important facts.
Perplexity's sources help — but skim them rather than trusting the summary blindly.
2
Don't paste sensitive information
These are public services. Your prompts may be used to improve the model. Avoid pasting personal data,
passwords, client info, or anything confidential. Use enterprise-tier accounts (Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT
Enterprise) for work data.
3
The landscape changes fast
These tools change month-to-month. New features, new models, new pricing tiers. Check in every few weeks —
your favourite tool may have caught up, or fallen behind.

## Page 18

### Summary — what to take away

There's no "one best AI tool"
Each tool has strengths. Try a few. Decide for yourself
which suits you.
Use sources
Perplexity, Copilot, and most chatbots cite sources in
2026. Click them. Skim them. Trust the original, not
the summary.
Match tool to task
Research → Perplexity. Quick explainer → ELI5.
Work in Office → Copilot. Long writing → Claude.
Conversation → ChatGPT.
Check in regularly
AI moves fast — a 6-month-old tip can already be
stale. A quick monthly look keeps you current.
