### AI lives on your phone now

Most adults in New Zealand now use AI more often on their phone
than on a laptop. That changes what AI on mobile means — it's not
just a chatbot in a browser.
On-device intelligence
Simple tasks run on the phone itself. Faster, private, works offline. Complex
queries hop to a secure cloud.
Agentic, not just answering
Modern phone assistants do tasks across apps — book a table, fill a form,
summarise a screen, draft a reply.
Multimodal by default
Point your camera, drop in a photo, paste a screen — the model sees text,
images, audio together.
AI assistants are now built into every major phone.

### Gemini Intelligence on Android

Announced 12 July 2026 at The Android Show. Gemini Intelligence is Google's agentic-AI layer for Android 17 — your phone does tasks, not
just answers questions.
Magic Pointer
Tap-and-hold anywhere on screen — Gemini explains,
summarises or translates the thing under your finger.
Create Your Widget
Describe a widget in plain language — "a glance at my next
workout" — and Gemini builds one.
Glowbar
Live, contextual overlays — a recipe step-by-step, a translated
menu, a route on the lock screen.
Proactive automation
Gemini moves across your apps to complete multi-step jobs —
message a friend, book a ride, reschedule a meeting.
Same shift on iPhone and Android: phones are moving from "ask a question, get an answer" to "hand off a task, it gets done".

## Page 8

### Perplexity AI — search that answers

Perplexity is a free AI search app for iPhone and Android. Instead of returning a list of
links, it reads several sources and compiles them into one easy-to-read answer —
with citations you can click.
1
Download & open
Available on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Free version works without a
subscription.
2
Skip the upsell
After login Perplexity offers a paid plan. Tap the × on the top left — you can always
upgrade later.
3
Ask anything
Tap the prompt bar at the bottom and type or speak your question — full sentences
work best.
Home screen → asking a question
Try first: "What is the most common bird in New Zealand?"

## Page 9

### Find the sources, ask follow-ups

AI can get facts wrong. Perplexity shows its working — and lets you keep refining without retyping the whole question.
Sources & Related panel
Sources tab
Tap to see the websites Perplexity pulled the answer from. Always worth a click before trusting a fact.
Rewrite with another model
Free users get a second model (Sonar Pro). Different models phrase things differently — useful for a second opinion.
Related questions
Below the answer, Perplexity suggests follow-ups. Tap one or write your own — it keeps the context of your previous
question.
Voice & camera input
Tap the mic for spoken questions, the camera icon to ask about something in front of you.
Practice prompt: "What are the reviews on the iPhone 17?" — then follow up: "What's the battery life like?"

### Spotify's AI features

Open Spotify, tap Search, and choose Made For You. The AI features have grown a lot since 2023 — there are now four tools worth
knowing.
The AI DJ
An AI radio host who picks three or four songs at
a time and talks between them. Tap the blue DJ
icon to skip to the next set.
Daylist
Your day in a playlist — Spotify updates it several
times a day based on what you usually listen to
at that hour.
AI Playlist (prompt-based)
Type a vibe — "songs for a Sunday drive through
the Coromandel" — and Spotify builds a fresh
playlist.
Daily Mixes & Artist Mixes
Long-running personalised mixes — by mood,
genre, decade, and the artists you already love.
The Made For You hub in Spotify
Heads up: Spotify needs listening history to learn your taste. Give it a week or two of regular use before judging the
recommendations.

## Page 14

### Hands-on: try it in 20 minutes

Pick one app from each of the three tools we covered. Spend roughly 6–7 minutes on each — long enough to see what's good and what's
clunky.
1
Search with Perplexity
Ask one fact-finding question. Tap into the sources panel. Then ask a follow-up that builds on the answer — without retyping the question.
2
Listen with Spotify AI
Open Made For You. Start the AI DJ for one set. Then try AI Playlist — describe the vibe in one sentence, see what it returns.
3
Make an image
Open ChatGPT, Gemini, or Canva on your phone. Describe a scene with style, mood and detail. Regenerate twice with small tweaks —
notice how each tweak changes the result.
Reflect: which task felt faster than your usual way of doing it? Which felt slower or wrong? Both answers are useful.

## Page 16

### Four things to take away

1
Your phone's assistant is a real AI now
Siri (Gemini-powered) and Gemini Intelligence are no
longer toy assistants. Ask them long, multi-step
questions and let them work across apps.
2
Use AI search for compiled answers
When you'd open five browser tabs, open Perplexity
instead. Check the sources panel before trusting a fact.
3
Personalisation gets better with use
Spotify AI, recommendation feeds and assistants all
improve as they learn what you like. Give them a few
weeks before judging.
4
Image generation is everywhere
You don't need a dedicated app — ChatGPT, Gemini,
Claude, Canva and Adobe Firefly all generate images
on mobile.
The best way to learn mobile AI is to use it for an hour, then again next week.
